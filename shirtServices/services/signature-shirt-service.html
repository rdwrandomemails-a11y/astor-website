SIGNATURE SHIRT SERVICE

Our signature shirt dry cleaning service is one of the premier offerings in NCR. With over 35 years of experience, we’ve been committed to keeping the city’s professionals looking clean, pressed, and impeccable. Trust us to deliver the exceptional care your shirts deserve.

Book A Slot

Exceptional Results, Uncompromising Quality

At Astor, we are renowned for our exceptional shirt cleaning standards. Our remarkable results stem from harmonizing traditional values with cutting-edge Italian Ironing techniques & Machines, employing organic cleaning agents that adhere to European Cleaning standards for the utmost care.

Upon your arrival at our state-of-the-art facility, your shirts will undergo a meticulous seven-stage garment care process. This thorough detailing allows us to meticulously inspect each garment for any imperfections or delicate embellishments before cleaning.

Next, we focus on stain removal, employing specialized techniques to identify and effectively treat stubborn stains. Finally, we conduct a comprehensive inspection to ensure the highest quality standards. Each shirt is hand-packed to guarantee a flawless service.

Environmentally friendly shirt cleaning service

At Astor, we’re dedicated to minimizing our environmental impact. We carefully select cleaning agents to reduce water & chemical usage and ensure the best possible results for your shirts.

We take pride in providing the finest shirt dry cleaning service Gurugram has to offer. For more information, contact our team on 813-0030385

Tradition meets technology

After the cleaning process, we use top-notch Italian technology and pressing equipment to iron your shirts. Our experts then meticulously hand-finish and inspect each shirt to ensure it meets our high standards.

As an Organic Dry Cleaner, we’re committed to reducing plastic usage and waste. All our bags are biodegradable, and we hand-pack every shirt, whether you prefer them hung or folded.

We minimize plastic use whenever possible and opt for easily recyclable, biodegradable packaging materials.
