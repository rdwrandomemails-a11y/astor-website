Wedding Dress Cleaning, Preservation and Gown Storage

The time, effort, and costs you invest in selecting your bridal attire don’t end on your wedding day. Afterward, you might want to have it dry cleaned to safely store your wedding dress and wear it again in the future.

At Astor, we take immense pride in providing the utmost attention and care that such special garments deserve. With over three and a half decades of experience in wedding dress cleaning and preservation, we have honed our skills to an unmatched level.

Before cleaning, our team meticulously examines the fabrics and characteristics of each gown to determine the most suitable treatment methods. Ornamental features like needlework, beads, and embellishments are carefully protected or removed before cleaning and meticulously restored afterward.

Choose our wedding dress preservation service and have your gown delicately wrapped and packed for future use.
