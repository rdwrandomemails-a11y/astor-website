Couture Cleaning

Couture garments are the ultimate expression of individuality and style, and they require specialized treatment to maintain their pristine condition.

With over 35 years of experience in couture cleaning, our skilled team is expertly trained to handle the most intricate fabrics, designs, and embellishments. We provide meticulous care for a wide range of garments.

We clean various materials, including silks, satins, detailed hand-finished items, fur, leather, and suede. Each piece undergoes a thorough inspection prior to cleaning, with careful attention to details such as beads, buttons, and sequins. If necessary, our specialists expertly remove and reattach delicate ornamental features.

By combining expertise and craftsmanship with cutting-edge eco-friendly technologies, Astor ensures your couture pieces receive impeccable care, keeping them in outstanding condition for a lifetime.
