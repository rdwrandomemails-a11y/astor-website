Astor Luxury Shoes & Bags Cleaning Service

At Astor Luxury Shoes & Bags Cleaning Service, we understand that your shoes and bags are more than just accessories; they're an extension of your style and personality. Our mission is to provide top-notch cleaning and restoration services that breathe new life into your favorite items, ensuring they look as good as new. With years of experience and a passion for preserving quality, our dedicated team is here to help you maintain the beauty of your footwear and bags.

Services Offered

Bag Cleaning

Handbag Cleaning : Gentle yet effective cleaning for leather, fabric, and designer bags.

Restoration Services : Repairing scuffs, scratches, and wear on bags to restore their original beauty.

Odor Removal : Techniques to eliminate unpleasant smells without damaging the material.

Shoe Cleaning

Deep Cleaning : Removing dirt, stains, and odors from all types of shoes, including sneakers, leather, suede, and canvas.

Stain Treatment : Specialized techniques for tough stains, ensuring your shoes look pristine.

Protection Treatments : Application of protective coatings to guard against future dirt and damage.

⁠Custom Services

Personalized Care : Tailored cleaning and restoration services based on your specific needs.

Consultations : Expert advice on the best care practices for maintaining your items.

Why Choose Us?

⁠Expert Team : Our professionals are trained in the latest cleaning techniques and use high-quality products to ensure the best results.

Eco-Friendly Products : We prioritize sustainability by using eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are safe for both your items and the environment.

Convenient Service : Easy online booking and a user-friendly website make it simple to schedule your cleaning services.

Satisfaction Guarantee : We stand by our work and strive to exceed your expectations. If you’re not happy, we’ll make it right.
